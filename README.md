Focus-Gate: Bio-Digital-Health  

Welcome to the > Focus-Gate Protocol < an open-source, socio-technological ecosystem connecting canine cognitive health, neurodivergent human support (ADHD - Attention Deficit Hyperactivity), and cutting-edge Edge-AI. 

Our mission is to translate multi-modal biological data into actionable, everyday interventions. We utilize the principle of "Fördern durch Fordern" (Challenge to Cherish) to reactivate the Locus Coeruleus, protect the blood-brain barrier (Claudin-5), and combat neuroinflammation in both humans and animals. 

The Vision & Social Impact This project is built on three pillars of accessibility and inclusion:

1. Open-Source Software & Hardware:** Fully accessible blueprints to prevent corporate gating or patent-trolling. Anyone can build, fork, and improve this system.
 
2. Inclusive Production (WfbM): The physical hardware matrix is designed to be manufactured by sheltered workshops for people with disabilities (Behindertenwerkstätten / WfbM), combining social impact with high-quality, sustainable local production. 

3. Translational Co-Regulation: A unique bond-building protocol where a neurodivergent handler (ADHD - Attention Deficit Hyperactivity) and a cognitive-declining pet (Dementia/Hearing Loss) train together, co-regulating their nervous systems through the same evolutionary conserved pathways.

System Architecture Focus-Gate bridges macro-behavioral interaction with micro-molecular research:


Hardware Specification: The "Sniff & Catch" Matrix 

The physical training core is a 3x3 modular board designed for easy CNC milling and assembly in sheltered workshops: 

Microcontroller: ESP32 / Raspberry Pi 4 (Wi-Fi & Bluetooth enabled). 

Sensors: 9x Capacitive Touch-Sensors behind an easy-to-wipe front plate. 

Visual Stimuli: 9x RGB-LED Rings for high-contrast tracking (essential for dogs with hearing loss).  Olfaktorik Stimuli: 4x Low-noise 12V Micro-Solenoid Valves connected to medical-grade scent cartridges (e.g., Chamomile, Peppermint) triggered by an internal silent micro-compressor.  

Software Stack & AI Integration Gemma 4 (E4B): Runs natively on-device (Edge-AI) via the handler's smartphone/tablet/MiniPC. Analyzes real-time video streams for Pupillometry(tracking Locus Coeruleus activation) and automatically scales game difficulty to prevent tonic overstimulation. 

AlphaFold 3 Pipeline: Used in the backend to model how specific nutraceuticals prevent the enzymatic degradation of Claudin-5.
